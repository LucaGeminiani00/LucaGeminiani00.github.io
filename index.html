About

I am a PhD researcher in the Methods for Big Data Lab at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), supervised by Nadja Klein.

I work on Bayesian optimisation, prior-data fitted networks and in-context learning. I am interested in applying these methods to scientific and engineering problems, such as in industrial manufacturing, engineering and bioelectronics.

Before my PhD, I worked at the European Central Bank for two years and completed my Master's degree in Statistics and Econometrics at Bocconi University, with a thesis on diffusion models for time series synthesis under the supervision of Carlo Baldassi.
